## Entregables
ReportedBalance anchor handler (phase 0 of [ALG.EVENTS.ORDER], [ALG.ANCHOR]): realDelta = Bᵣ − projected opening of k, then re-anchor; with several anchors in the same k all report realDelta against the same projected opening and the latest date (tie: greater id) re-anchors; level and term mode do not change. ActualPayment handler (phase 4, compare only, [ALG.ACTUAL]): marks row k as paid and, with a breakdown, computes realDelta per component (real − projected). Implements the [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR] installmentNumber rule: when installmentNumber is present, k = installmentNumber and the date is informative; for k < 1 or after the last installment of the calendar (rule 1 of [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR]; inherited events are not re-checked) a typed validation error is thrown; a late payment lands on the given installment; without it (ReportedBalance only) k is the first installment with due date ≥ date. buildPaths (original = terms only, real = real events + anchors + payments, scenario = real + hypothetical events with k > cutoffK per [ALG.PATHS.CUTOFF], compared by installment number, not date) exposing cutoffK, the per-row paid flag and realDelta per anchor and per component. compareSchedules returning interestSaved, monthsSaved, netSaving, endDate and totalPaid per [ALG.METRICS]; schedules of different currencies throw CurrencyMismatchError. The scenario path runs the hypothetical events as its own and inherits the real ones: runSchedule(terms, scenarioEvents, ctx, { inheritedEvents: realEvents }) (rule 3 of [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR]; ex10/scenario-payoff-before-future-real-event).

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)

- [ ] [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR]: an ActualPayment with installmentNumber k lands on row k whatever its paidDate (late-payment example); a ReportedBalance with an explicit installmentNumber different from its date's installment anchors at that number; installmentNumber below 1 or after the last installment of the calendar throws the typed validation error (inherited events are not re-checked); a ReportedBalance without it uses the first installment with due date ≥ date (tests)
