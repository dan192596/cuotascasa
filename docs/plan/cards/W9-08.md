## Entregables
Implement amended HU-04 in wizard step 4 and in the reported-balance form of «Datos reales»: ask first for the statement date, derive the installment with the [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR] rule (shown as «Corresponde a la cuota N, que vence el dd/mm/aaaa»), and offer «Este saldo es antes de pagar esa cuota / después de pagarla». «Después» stores the anchor as the balance before installment k+1; the stored model is unchanged. The installment number stays editable as an advanced option. Wizard and form share the derivation logic (one pure function in the feature with its spec, no new cross-feature import).
