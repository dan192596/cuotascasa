## Entregables
A hostile or accidental huge file must never be read whole into memory. One cap, MAX_DATA_FILE_BYTES = 20 MiB (20 * 1024 * 1024), applies to both paths; export it from one module of each owned area, with the same value and a comment with the reasoning below. (1) Backup import (data/backup/provide-backup.ts, previewImport): check `file.size` BEFORE `file.text()`; over the cap, return importError('INVALID_JSON') without calling text(), arrayBuffer() or stream(). The existing fixed Spanish text of INVALID_JSON («El archivo no es un respaldo válido: no se pudo leer su contenido.») is the user-facing message; do not add a code, because BackupImportErrorCode is a frozen contract (data/api.ts) mirrored by the UI of features/settings/backup (W5-07). (2) Drive download: RemoteFile carries no size (packages/sync/src/ports.ts and the Drive fake's `fields` allowlist are frozen), so the cap is on the response. In packages/sync/src/google-drive/provider.ts, downloadFile rejects with SyncError('InvalidRemote') when Content-Length is present and above the cap, before reading the body; otherwise it reads response.body as a stream, counts bytes and cancels the stream and rejects with SyncError('InvalidRemote') as soon as the count passes the cap (no Content-Length, or a lying one). Under the cap it decodes UTF-8 exactly as response.text() did. A network failure while reading stays SyncError('NetworkError'). InvalidRemote already maps to «No se puede leer la copia de Drive. Tus datos locales no cambiaron.» in data/sync/sync-status-text.ts, and the session already stops without saving or uploading on it (ADR-0008 decision 4). Touch data/sync/ only if a test or that text needs it. (3) Sizing: a spec builds a seeded synthetic backup at the high end of a household (e.g. 10 loans × 360 ActualPayments with breakdown, 360 ReportedBalances and 100 LoanEvents each, 20 scenarios × 50 events per loan, plus tombstones) and records in its comment the size of the plain JSON and of the encrypted envelope (base64, about 4/3); the cap must keep at least 2× headroom over the encrypted size (Opus estimate: about 6 MB plain and 8 MB encrypted, so 20 MiB). If the measured headroom is under 2×, stop and ask Opus.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] A Blob/File whose size is over the cap is rejected with INVALID_JSON and its text(), arrayBuffer() and stream() are never called (spy), and nothing is written
- [ ] A normal backup (the e2e synthetic backup fixture and the large synthetic one of the sizing spec) still previews and imports
- [ ] Drive download: Content-Length over the cap rejects with SyncError('InvalidRemote') without reading the body; a body that passes the cap without Content-Length (or with a smaller lying one) is cancelled as soon as it passes and rejects with InvalidRemote; at or under the cap the text is identical to response.text()
- [ ] Sync session and DriveSyncService tests show an oversize remote file stops the sync with the InvalidRemote status text, saving and uploading nothing
- [ ] packages/sync keeps coverage ≥ 95 %; the sizing spec records plain and encrypted sizes and asserts the 2× headroom
