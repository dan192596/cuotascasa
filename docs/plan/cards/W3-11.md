## Entregables
Signal stores implementing data/api.ts for loans (incl. create without anchor and createWithAnchor: Loan + ReportedBalance in one transaction), events, reported balances, payments, scenarios (activeScenarioId per loan) and settings. Cascading tombstones on loan delete, currency locked while any non-deleted LoanEvent, ReportedBalance, ActualPayment or Scenario of the loan exists, StorageHealth.requestPersist() called once after the first successful write, pendingChanges and changesSinceBackup exposed from the DataStore.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] TestBed tests with the memory adapter cover every store method; a meta-test compares tested methods with data/api.ts
- [ ] createWithAnchor writes both records or neither (injected failure)
- [ ] Deleting a loan tombstones its events, balances, payments and scenarios
- [ ] Changing currency returns a typed error while any non-deleted LoanEvent, ReportedBalance, ActualPayment or Scenario exists (one test per child type), and succeeds when the loan has no non-deleted children
- [ ] requestPersist is called exactly once, after the first successful write (spy on the StorageHealth token); pendingChanges and changesSinceBackup update on writes

## Notas
