## Entregables
Token values for 'libreta bancaria' (ink on paper) in light and dark from the approved palette, keeping the frozen token names. Source Serif 4 and JetBrains Mono (tabular-nums) self-hosted via @fontsource. Material M3 theme at compact density mapped to tokens. Tailwind v4 @theme mapping. ThemeService (system/light/dark), provideAppTheme() and cc-theme-toggle.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] A contrast test over the token values proves text pairs >= 4.5:1 and UI/graphic pairs >= 3:1 in both themes
- [ ] The frozen apps/web/src/design-contract/tokens.contract.spec.ts passes (token names unchanged in light and dark)
- [ ] A grep of dist finds no external font or CDN URL; a grep test finds no hard-coded colors outside apps/web/src/styles/
- [ ] On '/' ThemeService follows the system and writes nothing to storage; under /app cc-theme-toggle persists the choice through SettingsStore (injected from the route providers)
- [ ] cc-theme-toggle passes its frozen contract spec
