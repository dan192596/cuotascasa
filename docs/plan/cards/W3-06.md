## Entregables
MoneyPipe, LocalDatePipe and RatePipe; MoneyInput, DateInput and RateInput (in ui/money-input/) as Signal Forms custom controls following the golden pattern. RateInput: the user types a PERCENT with up to 4 decimals (e.g. '7.25') and the control emits the fraction string through the domain's percentToRate (exact decimal shift, '0.0725'), displaying stored values with rateToPercent; it is reused by the wizard (W4-07) and the simulator (W4-03). Values are decimal strings and LocalDate, never JS numbers or Date.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] MoneyPipe('1234.5','GTQ') gives 'Q 1,234.50' and USD gives 'US$ 1,234.50'; negatives and zero follow the spec
- [ ] A grep/lint test finds no Number(), parseFloat or unary + on money in owned dirs
- [ ] LocalDatePipe outputs dd/mm/yyyy; DateInput rejects 31/02/2027
- [ ] MoneyInput accepts '1,234.56', 'Q1234.5' and '1234'; it rejects more than 2 decimals and letters with exact Spanish messages
- [ ] Errors are wired through aria-describedby (component tests)
- [ ] RateInput: '7', '7.25' and '0.0001' emit '0.07', '0.0725' and '0.000001'; more than 4 decimals, negatives and letters are rejected with exact Spanish messages; a stored '0.0126' displays as '1.26' (component tests)
