## Entregables
Handlers registered through the frozen registry. RateChange supports RECALC_INSTALLMENT_KEEP_TERM (default), KEEP_INSTALLMENT_ADJUST_TERM and BANK_INSTALLMENT (with the bank's installment value) with negative-amortization detection, using the EngineContext helpers (derived term via remainingTerm(state), [ALG.TERM]). Both handlers run in phase 1 of the total order key [ALG.EVENTS.ORDER]. FixedChargeChange carries the complete list of fixed charges effective from k and replaces all previous charges, including future-dated ones, per [ALG.FIXEDCHANGE].

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] RECALC_INSTALLMENT_KEEP_TERM reproduces its algorithm example to the cent (level recomputed on remaining balance and term from the first due date on or after the effective date)
- [ ] KEEP_INSTALLMENT_ADJUST_TERM reproduces its example including the final short row, and throws NegativeAmortizationError when level <= HALF_UP_2(B*r)
- [ ] BANK_INSTALLMENT reproduces its example
- [ ] FixedChargeChange replaces the complete list from k: every previous charge stops, including a future-dated one, and sending the list without a charge removes it; totals change from k and balances never change ([ALG.FIXEDCHANGE])
- [ ] Events at the same k follow the total order key [ALG.EVENTS.ORDER] (k, phase, date, typeRank, id): in phase 1 RateChange (typeRank 0) applies before FixedChargeChange (typeRank 1), both before installment k is computed (test); a grep test fails if a level/term formula is implemented in owned dirs instead of calling the EngineContext helpers; coverage >= 95%
