## Entregables
Prepayment handler: its k follows [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR] (first installment with due date ≥ the event date) and it applies in phase 3, right after installment k ([ALG.PREPAY]); REDUCE_TERM | REDUCE_INSTALLMENT, optional FLAT | PERCENT commission, payoff cap. AdvanceInstallments(n) handler built on projectCapital.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] A prepayment's k follows [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR]: with paymentDay 15, a prepayment dated the 20th applies after the NEXT month's installment, while one dated the 15th applies after that month's installment; examples across month boundaries and END_OF_MONTH (tests)
- [ ] REDUCE_TERM and REDUCE_INSTALLMENT reproduce their examples to the cent
- [ ] FLAT and PERCENT commissions are HALF_UP_2, appear in the row, count in total paid and never reduce principal
- [ ] An amount >= balance is capped to payoff; the row shows the capped amount, the schedule ends that month and is flagged as payoff
- [ ] AdvanceInstallments(n) prepays exactly the sum of capital of the next n installments and the term shrinks by exactly n; grep test for helper usage; coverage >= 95%
