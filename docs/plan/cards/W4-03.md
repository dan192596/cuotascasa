## Entregables
cc-quick-simulator built with Signal Forms per the spec §9 «Simulador» table: monto (principal), tasa de interés anual i typed as a percent with RateInput (W3-06), without insurance and with the legend '+ 1.26 % seguros FHA' next to the field, plazo en años (integer 1–30; termMonths = años × 12), optional abono: monto + installment number k with 1 ≤ k < termMonths + modo REDUCE_TERM | REDUCE_INSTALLMENT, no commission, applied as a Prepayment ([ALG.PREPAY]) with its cap. Uses fha-gt@1 (f = 0.01 + 0.0026, FHA_GT_V1, END_OF_MONTH), GTQ, with no fixed charges. Results: the level installment (capital + interest + FHA insurance; no IUSI or property insurance and no separate 'cuota total'), the installment-1 breakdown (interest, insurance, principal), total intereses y seguros Σ(interest + insurance), the number of installments and the end date of the base plan, and with an abono: intereses ahorrados (interestSaved), meses ahorrados (monthsSaved), nueva fecha fin and, with REDUCE_INSTALLMENT, the new level; computed with domain buildSchedule/buildPaths/compareSchedules in the browser on interaction; the prerendered HTML contains the form and no results. firstDueDate = END_OF_MONTH of the month after Clock.today() (a landing-safe clock, not data/'s; injected clock in tests). A debounced polite aria-live summary. Under the results: 'Cifras estimadas; el banco tiene la última palabra. No es asesoría financiera.'.
