## Entregables
Opus rulings for three findings, written in ADR-0027 and reflected in their sources. (1) finanzas-B2: the «Plan original frente a lo real» comparison counts re-anchoring as saving. Decide between a presentation fix (show the anchor adjustment, the sum of realDelta.perAnchor, apart from the saving, with an explanatory note; [ALG.METRICS] unchanged) and a rule change. (2) finanzas-B1: netSaving includes fixed charges such as IUSI that continue after payoff. Decide between relabelling (state what the figure includes, plus a figure without fixed charges) and a rule or schema change (a FixedCharge attribute «continues after payoff», which touches ADR-0005 and the oracle). Also fix the «Interés ahorrado» label that includes insurance (finanzas-B7). (3) ux-B3/finanzas-B5: amend HU-04 so the wizard asks for the statement date first, derives the installment with [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR] and offers «antes / después de pagar la cuota de ese mes», converting to k or k+1 before saving (stored model unchanged); state whether [ALG.EVENTS.ANCHOR] needs a new example. If a rule in docs/algorithm.md changes, follow section 5 of docs/plan/README.md (oracle regeneration and lineage notices) and update the owns of W9-06 in plan.json before it is launched.
