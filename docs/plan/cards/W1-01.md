## Entregables
Period loop for original terms: r = (i+f)/12, level per [ALG.LEVEL], charge = HALF_UP_2((B*(i+f))/12), never B times an already-rounded r (r only feeds [ALG.LEVEL]), with the FHA_GT_V1 interest/insurance split and [ALG.PERIOD.SPLIT], SIMPLE per-component rounding with capital = level − interest − Σ insuranceⱼ ([ALG.PERIOD.SIMPLE]), [ALG.ZERO] handled before the general formulas, [ALG.DATES] due dates and the firstDueDate/paymentDay consistency check (InvalidInputError), fixed charges with effective dates added to the total only ([ALG.FIXED]), the last-row rule per [ALG.LAST] with financialCharge (charge in FHA_GT_V1, interest + Σ insuranceⱼ in SIMPLE) used by both [ALG.LAST.FIXED_TERM] and [ALG.LAST.DERIVED_TERM], currency carried by every schedule. Implements the EngineContext helpers levelPayment, remainingTerm(state: PeriodState) and projectCapital(state, n) per [ALG.TERM] (the only allowed implementation of those formulas; levelPayment is exported through index.ts) and yearlySubtotals ([ALG.YEARLY]). The period loop dispatches every event through the frozen registry in the total order key [ALG.EVENTS.ORDER]; a NotImplementedError thrown by a stub handler propagates to the caller and is never swallowed. buildSchedule works for original terms; buildSchedule with events is out of scope for W1-01 (it becomes correct as W2-03, W2-04 and W2-05 replace the stubs).
