## Entregables
Opus contract micro-card for three panel findings (docs/plan/implementation/w9/README.md, qa-B1, qa-B3, seguridad-B3). Every change is additive and keeps main green: the implementations land in W9-02 and W9-03. (1) DataStore.markSynced in packages/persistence/src/ports.ts and the LocalDataset port of packages/sync/src/ports.ts take what was uploaded, so a record written after local.save keeps counting as pending (suggested: markSynced(at, uploaded) with the entity:id + updatedAt keys that travelled in the file; the parameter is optional until W9-02 makes the session pass it, and its doc comment states the rule). (2) A new SyncErrorCode for a full Drive (suggested 'QuotaExceeded') for 403 storageQuotaExceeded, with the Spanish status text in data/sync/sync-status-text.ts («Tu Google Drive está lleno: libera espacio y vuelve a sincronizar») and the 403 reasons documented in docs/specs/drive-api-subset.md; dailyLimitExceeded is classified as a rate limit (NetworkError) and the decision is written there; the Google fakes in packages/sync/src/testing/ can answer a 403 with a given reason. (3) BackupService in data/api.ts: exportJson no longer marks the backup; a new method (suggested markExported(file)) marks it, and data/backup/provide-backup.ts implements it keeping the existing guard (a write notified after exportAll prevents the mark). (4) ADR-0008 decision 4 and ADR-0007 amended with the new rules; ADR-0024 is not changed.
