## Entregables
Steps: (1) plantilla y banco (template, bank as free text with no preloaded list, alias stored as Loan.name, currency); (2) condiciones originales: principal (> 0), termMonths (integer ≥ 1), interestRate (≥ 0), rateType (required; fha-gt@1 preloads VARIABLE), paymentDay (1–31 or END_OF_MONTH), firstDueDate (valid YYYY-MM-DD; changing paymentDay re-derives its day and an inconsistent pair is rejected with a Spanish message, [ALG.DATES]), disbursementDate (informative, ≤ firstDueDate), editable insuranceRates rows (labels per the glossary terms mortgageInsurance and lifeInsurance, each a decimal ≥ 0; rows can be removed and an 'otro seguro %' row can be added) and a roundingProfile select (FHA_GT_V1 | SIMPLE); interestRate = 0 with every insurance rate at 0 is allowed ([ALG.ZERO]). Every rate is typed as a PERCENT with up to 4 decimals in RateInput (W3-06) and stored as the fraction string by an exact decimal shift (domain percentToRate/rateToPercent, W0-03); (3) cargos fijos: level comes from the domain levelPayment (W0-03); the fixed charges derived from the bank total (bank total − level) get effectiveFrom = firstDueDate and an editable named breakdown (e.g. IUSI, seguro de daños) whose lines must sum to bank total − level (Spanish error otherwise); an empty bank total means no fixed charges; (4) optional saldo real ancla: balance (MoneyInput), installmentNumber (integer 1 ≤ k ≤ termMonths, required when a balance is entered), date defaulting to the due date of k (editable) and source = 'manual'; an out-of-range k shows a Spanish message from the domain's typed error; semáforo per [ALG.VALIDATE] (GREEN|AMBER|RED informs and never blocks saving; AMBER and RED show the suggested cause with Spanish labels: INSTALLMENT_MISALIGNMENT = 'El saldo parece corresponder a otra cuota (¿la anterior o la siguiente?)', UNKNOWN = 'Diferencia sin causa identificada: revisa tasa, seguros o cargos'). Saving calls LoansStore.createWithAnchor when an anchor is entered, otherwise LoansStore.create (no ReportedBalance; step 4/summary shows the status chip 'Sin validar'), and redirects to /app/prestamos/:id. Step components are exported for reuse by the detail screen. Plus the page object.

## Criterios de aceptación (objetivos, verificables por pruebas o CI)
- [ ] An invalid step blocks advancing with Spanish messages; going back keeps values
- [ ] Template values are copied and templateRef {id, version} stored; editing the copy leaves the template unchanged
- [ ] Fixed charges = bank total - level update live; a negative value shows an error
- [ ] A failed save writes nothing (createWithAnchor failure test)
- [ ] e2e (Chromium + WebKit): a synthetic GTQ loan with a matching balance shows GREEN and redirects; a mismatch shows RED; a USD loan displays US$
- [ ] Step 2 principal: empty, '0' and '-1' are rejected with Spanish messages; '0.01' is accepted
- [ ] Step 2 termMonths: 0, '1.5' and letters are rejected; 1 and 360 are accepted
- [ ] Step 2 interestRate: a negative value is rejected; '0' and a positive decimal are accepted
- [ ] Step 2 rateType: advancing without FIXED or VARIABLE is blocked; fha-gt@1 preloads VARIABLE
- [ ] Step 2 paymentDay: 0 and 32 are rejected; 1, 31 and END_OF_MONTH are accepted
- [ ] Step 2 firstDueDate: an impossible date (31/02/2027) is rejected; a valid date is stored as YYYY-MM-DD
- [ ] Step 2 disbursementDate: a date after firstDueDate is rejected, an equal date is accepted, and changing it does not change the schedule
- [ ] Step 4 is optional: saving without an anchor calls LoansStore.create, the store holds no ReportedBalance for the loan and step 4/summary shows the status chip 'Sin validar'
- [ ] AMBER and RED do not block saving and show the suggested cause before saving with its Spanish label (INSTALLMENT_MISALIGNMENT = 'El saldo parece corresponder a otra cuota (¿la anterior o la siguiente?)', UNKNOWN = 'Diferencia sin causa identificada: revisa tasa, seguros o cargos') (component test); an e2e saves a loan whose anchor is RED and redirects
- [ ] Step 2 insuranceRates: fha-gt@1 shows its rows with the glossary labels for mortgageInsurance and lifeInsurance; a negative or non-numeric rate is rejected with a Spanish message and '0' is accepted; a row can be removed; an 'otro seguro %' row can be added and is stored in insuranceRates (component tests)
- [ ] Step 2 roundingProfile: the select offers exactly FHA_GT_V1 and SIMPLE, is preloaded from the template, and advancing without a value is blocked with a Spanish message (component test)
- [ ] Rates are typed as percents: '7.25' is stored as '0.0725' and '1.26' as '0.0126' by exact decimal shift (percentToRate); more than 4 decimals is rejected with a Spanish message (component test)
- [ ] Changing paymentDay re-derives firstDueDate's day (END_OF_MONTH turns 2027-03-15 into 2027-03-31; paymentDay 15 turns 2027-03-31 into 2027-03-15); an inconsistent pair is rejected with a Spanish message ([ALG.DATES]); interestRate '0' with every insurance rate at '0' is accepted ([ALG.ZERO]) (component tests)
- [ ] Step 3: level comes from levelPayment; the derived fixed charges are stored with effectiveFrom = firstDueDate; named lines that do not sum to bank total − level show a Spanish error and block advancing; an empty bank total stores no fixed charges (component tests)
- [ ] Step 4: entering a balance requires installmentNumber; k = 0 or k > termMonths shows the Spanish message from the typed error; the date defaults to the due date of k and stays editable; the saved ReportedBalance has source 'manual' (component and store tests)
- [ ] After saving, the app redirects to /app/prestamos/:id and the e2e asserts the detail shell's data-testid='page-<route-id>'
